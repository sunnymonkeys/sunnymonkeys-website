A studio builtaround one idea.

The designer who builds your brand should be the one who stays with it.

A note from Marco, founder

I started Sunny Monkeys after watching the same thing happen again and again at agencies: a designer gets four or five brands at once, can't know any of them well, and the work drifts. The client notices. The designer notices. The model doesn't let anyone fix it.

A good brief is enough for a brand that's just getting started. So we do projects with a clear scope and a fixed price.

An established business needs someone who knows it inside out. So we built Your In-House Designer: one bilingual designer, dedicated to your brand, with our art direction behind them. Remote, but not outsourced.

"The designer who built your brand stays with it."

Marco Mendoza, founder

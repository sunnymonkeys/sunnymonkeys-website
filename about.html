A studio builtaround one idea.

The designer who builds your brand should be the one who stays with it.

A note from Marco, founder

I started Sunny Monkeys after watching the same thing happen again and again at agencies. A designer gets four or five brands at once. They do their best, but they can't know the context of each business, so every month the work looks a little different, and a little less like the company it's for. The client notices. The designer notices. Nobody can fix it, because the model doesn't allow it.

A good brief is enough for a brand that's just getting started. That's why we do projects with a clear scope and a fixed price: a brand identity, a website that works, the templates you need to show up every week.

But an established business needs more than a brief. It needs someone who knows it inside out. So we built the second half of the studio around that: Your In-House Designer. One bilingual designer, dedicated to your brand, working your hours inside your tools, with our art direction behind them. Remote, but not outsourced.

We use AI to move faster. The ideas, the judgment and the art direction are human.

"The designer who built your brand stays with it."

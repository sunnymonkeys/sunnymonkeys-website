Before you choose a package

  • Do you only work with home care agencies?

    Home care is the business we know best, and this site speaks to it. We still take on projects for other businesses.

  • Is the price really fixed?

    Yes. Every package is a one-time project with a defined scope, quoted before we start. Anything outside that scope gets its own quote first.

  • What do I pay after launch?

    The package is a one-time project. Your domain and hosting are paid by you, in your name, and we help you set them up. If you'd like us to look after the site, care plans start at $149 a month.

  • How long does a project take?

    Your proposal states the timeline before we start, along with the scope and the price. Premium comes with priority turnaround.

  • Who owns the work?

    You do. Every file and every design is yours, and your domain and hosting are in your name.

  • Can I update the website myself?

    Yes. Every package includes a website where you edit your own text and images. If you'd rather not, an in-house designer can keep it updated for you.

  • How many revisions are included?

    Starter includes one round, Growth two and Premium three, within the agreed scope. Nothing goes live without your approval.

  • What if I need something that isn't in a package?

    Tell us about your project and we'll put together a custom quote. That covers a team hub and custom web apps too: dashboards and internal tools.

  • Does the team hub hold client records?

    No. It holds team information only: time off, news, the handbook and forms. Client records stay in your care software.

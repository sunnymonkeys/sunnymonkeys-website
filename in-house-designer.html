In-House

Dedicated, not outsourced

The designer who built your brand stays with it

Your In-House Designer is a bilingual designer from our network in Latin America, dedicated to your business. They learn your brand, your values and how you operate, then take care of your design every week: social media, video editing, website updates, brochures, flyers and event materials. Remote, on your schedule, inside your tools, with Sunny Monkeys art direction behind every piece.

Full-time or part-time Start in 2 to 3 weeks Art direction included English and Spanish

Why not an agency?

One designer. Four or five brands. Something gives.

At most agencies, a designer handles four or five brands at the same time. They can't know the context of each business, so the work drifts: a different look every month, less consistency, less impact.

A good brief is enough for a brand that's just getting started. An established business needs someone who knows it inside out. That's what your in-house designer is: one person, dedicated to your brand, who gets better at your business every week.

Questions

Before you book the call

Is this outsourcing?

Remote, yes. Outsourced, no. Your designer works only for you (two brands at most on part-time), in your hours and your tools, with our art direction behind them. Think of it as a hire, without the hiring.

Where are the designers?

Bilingual designers in Latin America who work on US hours. Your designer speaks your team's language, in English and Spanish.

Can my designer build our new website?

A full new website is a project with its own scope and quote. Your designer keeps it updated afterwards. See our services and packages.

What's the minimum commitment?

Three months. Enough for the immersion to pay off and for your designer to really know your business.

What if it isn't working out?

Tell us. If your designer isn't the right fit, we'll match you with a new one.

Who owns the work?

You do. Every file and every design your designer creates is yours, and software licenses are included.
